## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Gurbani Search is a labor of love built to help the global Sangat connect with Gurbani through a high-performance, offline-first experience. Search across Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, Dasam Bani, and Vaaran Bhai Gurdas Ji with zero internet required.

Whether you want to search for a Shabad, read your daily Nitnem, explore Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji Ang-by-Ang, or track your spiritual goals, Gurbani Search provides a respectful, fast, and feature-rich companion for your journey.

========================================
✨ KEY FEATURES
========================================

🔍 POWERFUL & INTELLIGENT SHABAD SEARCH
• Gurmukhi First-Letter Search: Type word initials in Gurmukhi (e.g. 'ਸਸਗ' to find 'ਸੁਣਿਐ ਸਤੁ ਸੰਤੋਖੁ ਗਿਆਨੁ').
• English Phonetic Search: Search using Roman characters (e.g. 'kejjb' to find 'ਕਿਨਕਾ ਏਕ ਜਿਸੁ ਜੀਅ ਬਸਾਵੈ').
• Middle-of-Line Search: Search using continuous initials starting from anywhere in a Tukk (e.g. 'sgepjc' for 'ਚਰਨ ਸਰਨ ਗੁਰ ਏਕ ਪੈਂਡਾ ਜਾਇ ਚਲ').
• In-App Custom Keyboard: Switch seamlessly between Punjabi (Gurmukhi) and English phonetic keyboard layouts.
• Search History: Access and reload recent searches with a single tap.

📖 RESPECTFUL READING & CUSTOMIZATION
• Display Controls: Toggle Punjabi meanings, English meanings, Transliterations, Hindi text, Vishrams (colored pauses), and Larivaar (continuous script).
• Guided Recitation: Main Vishram pauses highlighted in Green and secondary pauses in Blue.
• Pinch-to-Zoom: Enlarge or shrink font size using a two-finger pinch gesture anywhere on the screen.
• Navigation Arrows: Easily jump to the previous or next Shabad in scripture sequence.

📁 PRAKARANS (CUSTOM SHABAD FOLDERS)
• Organize for Occasions: Create custom folders (e.g. Anand Karaj, Gurpurab, Keertan Routine, Morning Prayers).
• Save Shabads & Tukks: Save full Shabads or exact highlighted Tukks, with instant deep-linking when opened.

ੴ NITNEM & BANIS (DAILY ROUTINE)
• Custom Routine Ordering: Hold and drag any Bani up or down to match your personal Maryada routine.
• Sukhmani Sahib: Divided into 24 clean Ashtapadis with dark blue Salok styling and page-turning arrows.
• Full Banis Included: Japji Sahib, Jaap Sahib, Tav Prasad Savaiye, Chaupai Sahib, Anand Sahib, Rehras Sahib, Kirtan Sohila, Sukhmani Sahib, Aasa Ki Var, and more.

📜 SRI GURU GRANTH SAHIB JI (1430 ANGS)
• Complete Scripture Reader: Access full 1430 Angs directly inside the Banis tab.
• Ang-by-Ang Navigation: Turn Angs smoothly using bottom arrows or tap 'Ang X / 1430' to type an Ang number and jump instantly.

📊 NITNEM TRACKER (SPIRITUAL PROGRESS)
• Goal Templates: Setup progress trackers for Mool Mantar Jaap, Waheguru Simran, Bani counts, or Sehaj Path.
• Flexible Units: Log progress in Raw Units, Maalas (1 Maala = 108 units), or Angs.
• Flexible Duration: Set fixed target dates or choose 'No End Date' for continuous daily tracking.
• Past Date Logging: Easily back-log progress for past dates if you missed a day.
• Live Analytics: View live progress bars, daily averages, expected targets, and color-coded status badges (Ahead, On Track, Behind).

♿ ACCESSIBILITY & READABILITY
• 24/7 Light Theme Lock: High-contrast light interface immune to device dark mode shifts.
• A-Z Bold Text Mode: Dedicated toggle in About page to make all text across the app bold for easier reading.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST & 100% OFFLINE
• No Accounts Required: No sign-ups, no logins, no personal data collection.
• Zero Ads & Zero Tracking: No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no data sharing. Your spiritual progress remains strictly on your device.

May this small tool be a companion on your spiritual journey.
```
